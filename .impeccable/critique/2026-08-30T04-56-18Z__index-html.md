# Design Specificity Verdict

Split, and split backwards. The page is authored wherever it handles FACTS and
interchangeable wherever it handles CLAIMS — the opposite of what a Persuade
surface needs, because the claims are what a first-timer meets first.

Genuinely authored: .shopgrid (seven real photographs, every dessert shot
against the shop's own flower wall, so room and food read as one place); the
hours table (split Friday as two stacked times in one row, Monday tinted with a
CLOSED pill); the pickup panel ("Nothing is paid for here"); the footer drip SVG
echoing the actual drip-painted counter in shop-counter.jpg.

Category-interchangeable: the entire hero copy block — "Premium treats, made
just for you / Freshly made desserts and drinks." Swap the photo for a croissant
and nothing becomes false. The four hero badges and the Why Us grid then restate
three of the same four unproveable claims.
